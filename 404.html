About me

I am an Associate Professor at the Maersk Mc-Kinney Moller Institute and the SDU Center for Industrial Software, University of Southern Denmark.

I lead the Applied and Interpretable Machine Learning Research Group and co-chair the Data and Intelligence Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of AI interpretability, representation learning, and scalability of intelligent systems. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Kent State University. My broader interests include algorithmics, knowledge graphs, distributed systems, and applied artificial intelligence.
